# Independent Publishing Business — Baseline Market Landscape Study
**Date range:** 7 Oct 2026  
**Researcher:** Senior publishing-market analyst  
**Workbook source:** Publishing-Companion-v1-1_Baseline-Research-Universe.xlsx – 55 book candidates (BOOK-01 to BOOK-55) + 20 direct-digital hypotheses (DIG-01 to DIG-20)  
**Methodology:** Workbook Research_Routine (7 steps, ~85 min nominal, extended for baseline), Rubric scoring, 48-result saturation screen, BSR / reviews / freshness / defect gap / royalty / production fit / cluster / cross-channel criteria. Evidence preserved in Scoring sheet columns AY/AZ.

## 1. Research purpose, methodology, limitations
The purpose was to build a one-time evidence-backed baseline before any production decision. All 75 hypotheses were researched on Amazon.com as primary for KDP paperback, with channel-native checks for digital products (Etsy, TPT, Creative Fabrica, Payhip/Gumroad). Beekeeping Hive Inspection Log (BOOK-01) was retained as calibration case per brief.

Limitations:
- BSR is a relative rank, not sales; conversion to units not attempted. Where JS-blocked, category rank used as proxy.
- Review themes limited to visible Amazon 1-3 star snippets; deeper mining requires Look Inside and account-level review filtering.
- Keyword volume tools unavailable; search intent inferred from autocomplete and buying words (log, workbook, tracker).
- For low-content, generic cover count assessed visually from first 2 pages (~48 results). Count is subjective but comparable across niches.
- Digital marketplace sales data is opaque; favorites, sales badges, and review counts used as indirect proxies.
- Policy language changes frequently; all policy checks dated 7 Oct 2026.

## 2. Overall market-landscape findings
- **Low-content utility (Family A):** 25 candidates. Market is hyper-saturated (35-44/48 generic covers). Top 3 BSR typically 250k-800k, avg reviews 2-60. Only niches with compliance or resale-value incentive (marine log, RV, beekeeping, poultry, vegetable garden) retain High intent. Aquarium, fishing, cleaning business, handyman, small farm equipment show BSR >700k-2.5M and very low reviews, indicating weak demand or excessive supply.
- **Medium-content workbooks (Family B):** 17 candidates. BSR healthier (45k-350k) but dominated by branded publishers (Spectrum, Happy Fox, Chess 1001 series). Defect gap is the moat: Strong gaps (missing answer keys, errors, no progression) appear in fractions/decimals, multiplication/division, multi-step word problems, reading comprehension. Production effort rises to Partial/Low fit – requires original exercises, answer keys, illustrations.
- **Flagship/high-content (Family C):** 13 candidates. BSR 80k-350k, better royalty ($4.5-5.0 net for 200-page 6x9 at $16.99-19.99). Highest differentiation potential but editorial accuracy surface Medium-High. Strongest: First-Time Homeowner Seasonal Maintenance Playbook (BOOK-09), Practical Beekeeping First-Year Guide (BOOK-44), Small Boat Ownership Guide (BOOK-43), RV Ownership First-Year (BOOK-46), First-Time Landlord Playbook (BOOK-51), Balcony Gardening Guide (BOOK-54).
- **Direct digital (Family D):** 20 candidates. Primary channels split: TPT for education (DIG-08 to DIG-13, DIG-17 classroom adapt), Etsy for printable logs/activity (DIG-01,02,04,05,06,11,17,19,20), Payhip/Gumroad for B2B toolkits (DIG-03,07,14,15,16), Creative Fabrica for vector assets (DIG-18). Egypt payout updated: Etsy Payments IS available in Egypt via Payoneer account (star-marked country)[^1], not blocked. Google Play seller sign-ups for Egypt is ★ – only select partners, new partners not accepted at this time[^2], meaning existing account is grandfathered but cannot be counted on for new publishers. KDP supports Egypt with EFT/bank transfer threshold.

## 3. Summary of candidate families

### Family A – Utility low-content
- Beekeeping (BOOK-01) calibration: Avg BSR 180k, reviews 85, generic 12/48, defect Some (typos, limited hive numbers, missing queen cells/food stores). Royalty $3.0. Tier calculation ~58-62 (Tier 2 watchlist) due to saturation but clear defect gap.
- Marine Engine (BOOK-02): BSR ~450k, saturation 32/48, but high intent for resale/service history. Cluster with BOOK-43 flagship.
- Sourdough (BOOK-05): BSR ~600k, generic 38/48, 745 results for sourdough journal, low velocity.
- RV (BOOK-06): BSR ~250k, freshness Yes – new entrants <6 mo with <100 reviews and BSR <100k observed, good cluster potential.
- HVAC (BOOK-07): BSR ~300k, reviews only 4 avg, generic 36/48, but Strong defect gap (missing EPA refrigerant fields, no signature) – B2B compliance opportunity.

Weakest in Family A: Aquarium (BOOK-16) BSR 773k-11M, reviews 8, generic 42/48; Fishing (BOOK-17) BSR 1.1M-4.9M; Cleaning Business (BOOK-21) BSR 2.48M; Handyman (BOOK-22) BSR 2.57M; Small Farm Equipment (BOOK-25) BSR 665k.

### Family B – Medium-content workbooks
- Road Trip Activity 8-12 (BOOK-08): BSR 45k, reviews 200, freshness Yes, seasonal summer peak, but Happy Fox dominance (786 ratings). Medium return risk (gift).
- Fractions & Decimals 5-6 (BOOK-03): BSR 350k KDP-only, but branded Spectrum #478 overall – entrenched. Strong defect gap (no answer key, errors) offers moat if original.
- Chess Tactics (BOOK-04): BSR 300k KDP, but 1001 series 795 reviews 4.6★ – high social proof barrier.
- Large-Print Logic Puzzles Adults (BOOK-32): BSR 120k, freshness Yes – low-content adjacent but puzzle creation needs originality to avoid repetitive content flag.
- Backyard Birdwatching Activity Book Kids (BOOK-35): BSR 90k, High intent, cluster with birding family.

Saturated workbook areas: Beginner Calligraphy (BOOK-33), Music Theory (BOOK-34) – generic 22-24/48, low reviews but high production illustration burden.

### Family C – High-content and digital-first
Strongest evidence:
- BOOK-09 Homeowner Playbook: BSR 80k, royalty $4.5, cluster 5+ titles, cross 3+ channels – evergreen homeowner pain.
- BOOK-44 Practical Beekeeping Guide: BSR 90k, complements BOOK-01 log, high research burden but publisher has domain interest.
- BOOK-51 First-Time Landlord Playbook: BSR 120k, royalty $4.7, 5+ cluster, distinct from generic rental ledgers.
- BOOK-54 Balcony & Small-Space Gardening: BSR 80k, reviews 150, growing urban trend.
- BOOK-47/48 Freelance Designer Playbooks: BSR 200k-250k, Strong defect gap (missing workflow), high engine fit with publisher's graphic design experience, and direct-digital toolkit opportunity (DIG-14).

Risky high-content: BOOK-10 Birdwatching Field Guide (BOOK-10) requires ornithology expertise and photo licensing – editorial High, and competes with NatGeo #8,190 Books.

### Family D – Direct digital
- Education (DIG-08,09,10): TPT top bundles 2k-10k sales at $4-8, 30-45% commission. Originality requirement is vetting – AI-generated passages flagged. Production High but aligns with Book Studio.
- B2B Toolkits (DIG-14 Designer Onboarding, DIG-15 Publisher Dashboard, DIG-07 HVAC forms, DIG-16 Work-Order): Payhip 0-5% + PayPal, Gumroad 10%. Demand smaller but price $19-49, better margin, and strong fit with publisher skills. Egypt viable via PayPal.
- Home/RV/Bird printables: Etsy high volume but 38-42/48 generic. Differentiation via compliance fields, photo slots, seasonal grouping.

## 4. Candidate ranking using workbook rubric
Rubric weights: Demand 25 (BSR pts 10, intent 8, freshness 7), Competition 25 (reviews 10, defect 8, saturation 7), Economics 20 (royalty 8, eBook fee 7, return 5), Feasibility 15 (engine fit 8, editorial 7), Ecosystem 15 (cluster 8, cross-channel 7). Tier 1 ≥75, Tier 2 ≥60.

Calculated totals (from working copy):
- Tier 2 watchlist candidates (60-74): Beekeeping Log (BOOK-01) ~64, RV Log (BOOK-06) ~68, HVAC Log (BOOK-07) ~66, Homeowner Playbook (BOOK-09) ~71, Bird Observation Log (BOOK-18) ~62, Fractions Workbook (BOOK-03) ~62, Road Trip Activity (BOOK-08) ~70, First-Time Landlord (BOOK-51) ~69, Balcony Gardening (BOOK-54) ~67, Freelance Designer Playbooks (BOOK-47/48) ~65, Publisher Guide (BOOK-50) ~63.
- Tier 3 (<60): Aquarium (BOOK-16) ~32, Fishing (BOOK-17) ~35, Cleaning Business (BOOK-21) ~28, Handyman (BOOK-22) ~30, Small Fleet (BOOK-12) ~38, Motorcycle (BOOK-13) ~40, Small Farm Equipment (BOOK-25) ~36, Sourdough Log (BOOK-05) ~42.
No candidate reaches Tier 1 ≥75 in this baseline due to saturation penalty and low BSR points for low-content – indicating need for re-angling and deeper validation, not automatic production.

## 5. Strongest individual candidates and evidence
1. **BOOK-09 First-Time Homeowner Seasonal Maintenance Playbook** – BSR 80k, $4.5 net royalty, 5+ cluster, defect Some (most competitors are checklist logbooks, not seasonal playbook with explanations). Evidence: Ultimate Guide #7,577 Books, Home Maintenance for Dummies #41,247.
2. **BOOK-06 RV Trip, Inspection & Maintenance Logbook + BOOK-46 RV Ownership First-Year Guide** – Log BSR 250k with freshness Yes (new spiral planner BSR 80k-200k), guide BSR 100k. Cluster 5+ with DIG-04/20.
3. **BOOK-01 Beekeeping Hive Inspection Log (calibration) + BOOK-44 Practical Beekeeping First-Year Guide** – Log defect gap documented (typos, missing queen cells), saturation only 12/48 (lower than other logs). Guide BSR 90k.
4. **BOOK-07 HVAC Service Call & Equipment Maintenance Log** – Very low reviews (avg 4) but Strong defect gap: missing refrigerant tracking EPA compliance fields – B2B niche with low competition from branded publishers.
5. **BOOK-51 First-Time Landlord Playbook** – Adjacent to rental log but flagship guide has better economics ($4.7 royalty) and 5+ cluster with DIG-03.

## 6. Weak, saturated, risky, uneconomic candidates
- **Aquarium, Fishing, Cleaning Business, Handyman, Small Fleet, Motorcycle, Small Farm Equipment**: BSR >600k-2.5M, reviews <10, generic >38/48, Low intent or gift-driven, no defect gap. Veto risk: generic no specialized use.
- **Sourdough Log, Woodworking Log**: 38-40/48 generic, BSR 600k+, low velocity, easily replicated.
- **Low-content flagged by KDP policy**: KDP enforcement trends 2026 flag repetitive interiors and mass-upload models and increased scrutiny on repetitive or low-value content. Low-content must have meaningful differentiation, not just template swap.
- **High-content risk**: Birdwatching Field Guide (BOOK-10) requires extensive fact-checking and photo rights; Music Theory (BOOK-34) and Calligraphy (BOOK-33) require custom illustration – Low engine fit.

## 7. Strongest three product clusters to investigate first

### Cluster 1 – Homeownership & Rental Property Operations (Core: BOOK-09, BOOK-51, BOOK-24, BOOK-11)
- Audience: First-time homeowners, small landlords (1-5 doors), US suburban.
- Problem: Seasonal maintenance forgetfulness, turnover cost documentation for deposit/tax.
- Flagship: BOOK-09 Seasonal Maintenance Playbook (200p, 7x10, $19.99).
- Companion: BOOK-51 Landlord Maintenance & Turnover Playbook (flagship), BOOK-24 Home Inventory & Warranty Record Book (low-content), BOOK-11 Rental Turnover Log (low-content).
- Direct digital: DIG-01 Home Maintenance Binder (Payhip/Etsy via Payoneer), DIG-02 Inventory Spreadsheet, DIG-03 Turnover PDF Kit (B2B Payhip).
- Channels: KDP paperback primary, Google Play eBook for flagship (existing account grandfathered – Egypt seller sign-up now ★ only select partners[^2]), Payhip/Gumroad for kits.
- First to investigate/produce: DIG-01 binder – low production, tests demand, builds list; then BOOK-09 playbook.

### Cluster 2 – Beekeeping / Apiculture (Core: BOOK-01, BOOK-44, DIG-05)
- Audience: Hobby/small-scale beekeepers US/EU.
- Problem: Inspection records lack queen cells, brood pattern, food stores, treatment dosage – documented defects.
- Flagship: BOOK-44 Practical Beekeeping First-Year Hive Management Guide.
- Companion: BOOK-01 Hive Inspection Log (improved interior solving defects), queen rearing log, honey harvest log.
- Direct digital: DIG-05 Inspection & Honey Harvest Form Pack (Etsy/Payhip).
- Channels: KDP paperback (both), Etsy via Payoneer (Egypt eligible[^1]), Payhip.
- First: BOOK-01 revised interior – templatable with Python generator, low effort, calibration data exists.

### Cluster 3 – RV Ownership & Travel (Core: BOOK-06, BOOK-46, DIG-04, DIG-20)
- Audience: RV owners (US large market), first-year owners.
- Problem: Pre-trip inspection, seasonal storage, campsite records fragmented across generic logs.
- Flagship: BOOK-46 RV Ownership First-Year Setup, Safety Checks & Maintenance Guide.
- Companion: BOOK-06 Trip, Inspection & Maintenance Logbook (3-in-1), camping/hiking log (BOOK-23).
- Direct digital: DIG-04 Pre-Trip Inspection Bundle, DIG-20 Trip Planning & Campsite Bundle.
- Channels: KDP paperback, Etsy (RV checklists 600+ listings but gap in complete bundle), Payhip.
- First: DIG-04 bundle – low production, high Etsy favorites proxy.

Alternative clusters: Freelance Designer Workflow (BOOK-47,48,49 + DIG-14,15) – strong fit with publisher's graphic design background and B2B pricing $19-49, but smaller TAM; Small Boat Ownership (BOOK-02,43) – high intent but BSR 450k and seasonal.

## 8. Product-to-channel findings

**KDP (Amazon.com primary):**
- Egypt supported for payments[^3]. EFT no minimum threshold, check $100. Payout via bank transfer/wire, not PayPal.
- Low-content policy: Must tick low-content checkbox; must have meaningful content. Repetitive templates flagged. Generic notebooks excluded per brief – enforced.
- New upload limit 2 per format per week (since Sept 21 2026) – yearly ceiling 104 per format.
- Royalty: paperback 6x9 120p $7.99-9.99 net $2.5-3.4; 7x10 200p $16.99-19.99 net $4.5-5.0. eBook 70% band $2.99-12.99.

**Google Play Books:**
- Existing account with tax/bank configured – operational status should be tested with first upload.
- Country table: Egypt EG seller sign-ups ★ (e-book only)[^2] meaning only select partners, Google doesn't accept new partners currently. Payments to Egypt via wire in USD (★ USD). Supported countries for selling include Egypt for purchases ✔ (e-book only). So existing account is viable, but new Egypt publishers cannot rely on it.
- Payment threshold $100 USD via bank transfer, monthly 15th.

**Draft2Digital:**
- No upfront formatting fees, but as of 2026 new $20 activation fee and $12 annual maintenance if <$100 royalties. Commission 10-15% after retailer cut. Distributes to Apple Books, Kobo, B&N, OverDrive. Useful for Apple Books/Kobo Writing Life without ITIN via D2D.
- Egypt payout: via PayPal/bank – D2D supports international via PayPal.

**Apple Books, Kobo Writing Life, B&N Press:**
- Apple Books via D2D or direct – direct requires ITIN for US tax; Egypt not in direct sign-up list but via aggregator OK.
- Kobo Writing Life: Global reach 190 countries, authors from 157 countries[^4], Egypt mentioned as accessing Worldwide storefront when no specific storefront[^5] – so Egypt authors can publish, sales via Kobo worldwide.
- B&N Press: US-centric, but via D2D accessible.

**Etsy:**
- Etsy Payments now available in Egypt via Payoneer account[^1] and requires Payoneer wallet for Egypt[^6]. Earlier August 2025 migration forced all sellers to Etsy Payments[^7]. So Egypt seller is eligible, contrary to earlier assumption of not eligible – corrected.
- Fees: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, 3%+ payment.

**Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT):**
- Hyperwallet payout supports Egypt per seller reports; commission 30% premium /45% free. Strong for DIG-08 to DIG-13.

**Payhip/Gumroad:**
- Payhip 5% free plan or $0 with 0% on paid plan + Stripe/PayPal fees; Gumroad 10%. Both PayPal-based – Egypt PayPal receiving supported for goods/services since 2021. Viable zero-upfront-cost.

**IngramSpark, Lulu:**
- Print cost higher, setup fees $49-99 often waived with promos, but distribution via Ingram – only if KDP expanded distribution insufficient. Not priority for low-content.

**Policy principle:** Distinguish automated interior layout from auto-generated content. KDP allows Python-generated structured interiors if editorially reviewed and differentiated, but mass-produced repetitive interiors flagged as spam. For each candidate, channel fit marked uncertain where policy ambiguous – recommend written confirmation for borderline low-content.

## 9. Production implications

**A. Low-Content Generator (existing Python):**
- Full fit (8 pts): BOOK-01,02,06,07,11,14,15,18,19,20,23,24,25, plus aquarium/fishing etc – but ease ≠ opportunity. Generator reduces time but does not solve saturation.
- Best leverage: Beekeeping (defect-driven fields), RV (3-in-1 with inspection checklists), Home Inventory (warranty index), HVAC (compliance fields). These need custom field design, not generic.

**B. Digital Book Studio (planned AI-assisted eBook):**
- Partial fit: All medium-content workbooks (BOOK-03,26,27,28,30) and digital-first eBooks (BOOK-45 sourdough troubleshooting, BOOK-50 publishing workflow). Requires human editorial control for answer keys, curriculum alignment, original passages.
- Human judgment priority: Reading comprehension (BOOK-28) must be original stories to avoid IP; math workbooks need progressive difficulty and worked examples – AI can draft but must be verified.

**C. High-Content Book Studio (planned research-intensive):**
- Needed for flagship guides (BOOK-09,43,44,46,51,52,53,54,55) and designer playbooks (BOOK-47-50). Requirements: research dossiers, source tracking, fact-checking, diagrams/photos.
- Production complexity Low for logs, Medium for workbooks, High for flagship guides.
- Graphic design advantage: Publisher can differentiate covers and interior usability – important where generic look-alike >30/48.

## 10. Recommended initial production sequence
No publication authorized, but research sequence for validation:

1. **DIG-01 Home Maintenance Binder + DIG-02 Spreadsheet** – Zero-cost test via Payhip, validates Cluster 1 demand, builds email list, low effort.
2. **BOOK-01 Revised Beekeeping Hive Inspection Log** – Uses existing generator, solves documented defects, calibration case, revalidate BSR 180k baseline.
3. **BOOK-09 Outline & Sample Chapter (First-Time Homeowner Playbook)** – Research dossier, not full book; test Google Play existing account with first eBook upload (short guide) to confirm payout operation.
4. **DIG-14 Freelance Designer Onboarding Toolkit** – Leverages publisher expertise, B2B price $29-39, Payhip test.
5. **If early signals positive:** BOOK-44 Practical Beekeeping Guide or BOOK-51 Landlord Playbook as first flagship (150-200 pages).

Sequence respects zero-low upfront cost, uses existing KDP + Google Play + Payhip, and defers high-illustration workbooks.

## 11. Important unresolved questions
- Google Play Books existing account operational status – does payout still work for Egypt after ★ restriction? Test upload needed.
- KDP low-content threshold for Python-generated interiors with custom fields – will Amazon consider 120-page inspection log with 8 custom fields per page as sufficiently differentiated? Need written confirmation if BSR improves.
- TPT Hyperwallet Egypt payout current status – seller forum reports yes, but official list needed.
- Draft2Digital new fees impact on low-royalty titles – $12 annual if <$100 may make long-tail uneconomic.
- Actual sales vs BSR conversion for low-content niche – need own sales data to calibrate Rubric BSR points.
- Digital marketplace sales transparency – Etsy/TPT do not expose sales; reliance on favorites/ratings proxy limits confidence.

## 12. Suggested improvements to research rubric for next baseline cycle
- Add field for publication date of top 3 competitors (freshness signal currently dropdown only).
- Split generic look-alike count into cover vs interior template duplication (cover 48 screen + interior preview check).
- Add return risk data source – currently judgment only; pull KDP returns report after first titles.
- Add separate veto for AI-generated text without disclosure – KDP now requires AI content disclosure checkbox (2026).
- Add channel eligibility score (0-5) for Egypt payout – separate from economics.
- For digital products, replace BSR scoring with marketplace-native metrics (Etsy favorites, TPT sales band, Gumroad sales count) – do not force Amazon rubric onto Etsy.
- Preserve evidence links as separate Evidence Appendix CSV with candidate code, query, date, URL, ASIN, observed metric, screenshot note – workbook cells too small for full evidence.
